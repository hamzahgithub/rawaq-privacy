Scope and developer services

This policy describes Rawaq, Android package app.rawaq.media, published by the publisher named above. Rawaq does not require a Rawaq account. The reviewed app has no developer-operated analytics, advertising SDK, ad-identifier permission or automatic developer telemetry endpoint. This does not mean websites and other destinations receive no information when you use online functions.

Data stored on your device

Rawaq stores download source URLs, titles, progress, settings, bookmarks, recent links and local playlist references on the device. Partial downloads and working media originals are in app-specific storage. Completed media is published to Download/Rawaq by default, or to a folder you select. Playlist downloads may use a named subfolder. The app retains a working original in addition to the shared file. Android document grants allow access to files/folders you select. Local playback and conversion do not upload your files to a developer service.

Network requests and external services

Inspection, searching, downloading and thumbnails contact the selected website, its media/CDN hosts, and services used by its media extractor. These recipients can receive your IP/network address, requested URLs or queries, and the request information needed to serve the content. Browser address-bar searches go to DuckDuckGo. External websites and services operate under their own policies and may retain their own logs; Rawaq cannot determine or erase their retention from your device.

The app verifies HTTPS certificates/hostnames where HTTPS is used. A user-supplied HTTP source may still be processed by the native downloader; not every possible media transfer is encrypted. The app is not an encrypted media vault. Authorized media/file apps can access shared completed files, subject to Android permissions and device behavior.

Browser and optional website sign-in

The built-in browser is ordinary WebView browsing, not a private mode. Websites can use cookies and site storage and may show their own ads. Rawaq does not add ads to its own interface or promise to block website ads. The browser does not grant websites camera, microphone, location or file-upload access through Rawaq's browser permission handlers.

Site sign-in is optional. You sign in on the site's official HTTPS page; there is no Rawaq password form or password-reading JavaScript bridge. Website cookies and storage remain in the app's local WebView storage. A separate switch enables use of that site's saved session for downloads. The engine then receives a temporary, private cookie snapshot restricted to the applicable site hosts. It is not sent to a developer server, thumbnail hosts or arbitrary unrelated destinations. The selected website still receives the session information required for its authenticated request. Clearing a local session does not delete the external website account.

Temporary data, retention and deletion

Eligible public inspection results may be reused in process memory for up to 90 seconds, with at most four entries and an 8MiB combined limit. Account-enabled or authorization-bearing results are excluded. Temporary operation metadata and cookie snapshots are stored in owner-restricted private files and normally removed after their operation. A crash or cleanup failure may leave private files for later stale-file cleanup; immediate erasure is not guaranteed.

Use the app's controls to clear recent links, bookmarks or browser/site sessions. Removing a download from the list keeps its files. Deleting the downloaded file removes its Rawaq working original and primary published file after confirmation; independent copies are kept. Imported files stay at their original locations. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes app-private records and grants, while published shared files and copies can remain and must be deleted separately through the app or your file manager. Android backup/device transfer of app data is disabled.

Sharing and support

When you choose Share or Save a copy, the chosen destination/app receives the selected file or copy. Its handling is controlled by that destination. If you contact support yourself, the publisher and selected email/support provider receive the message and attachments you send. Do not send passwords or website cookie values. Support messages may remain in the publisher’s mailbox while the inquiry and follow-up are handled. To request deletion of a support message, email the support contact above. Any retention needed for an unresolved dispute or legal obligation must be explained to the requester. The email provider manages its own service records under its policies.

Questions and changes

Contact the privacy/support address above for questions about this policy or local deletion controls. The publisher cannot remotely erase device-local or third-party files without an applicable service. Material changes to Rawaq's data practices will be reflected in this policy and any required in-app notice. No Rawaq account-deletion endpoint is provided because the reviewed build has no Rawaq account creation; this must be reassessed if that feature changes.
